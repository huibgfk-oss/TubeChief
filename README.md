# TubeChief

A privacy-first browser extension that lets you manage YouTube likes, subscriptions, and playlists locally, without requiring a YouTube account.

## Features

- Like and organize videos locally
- Subscribe to channels without a YouTube account
- Create and manage local playlists
- Keep your preferences stored on your own device
- No external account required
- No unnecessary data sharing
- Fast and lightweight browser integration

## Privacy

TubeChief is designed with privacy in mind.

Your local TubeChief data stays on your device and is not sent to external servers for profile tracking or account synchronization.

You remain in control of your local subscriptions, likes, and playlists.

## How It Works

TubeChief adds an independent local management layer to YouTube.

Instead of relying on a YouTube account for basic organization features, TubeChief stores supported preferences locally in your browser.

This allows you to build your own local YouTube experience while keeping your data under your control.

## Installation

### From a Browser Extension Store

Store links will be added when TubeChief becomes available.

### Manual Installation

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open your browser's extensions page.
3. Enable **Developer Mode**.
4. Choose **Load unpacked**.
5. Select the TubeChief extension directory.

For Chrome:

```text
chrome://extensions/
```

For Microsoft Edge:

```text
edge://extensions/
```

## Usage

After installing TubeChief, open YouTube normally.

TubeChief provides local controls for supported actions such as:

- Likes
- Channel subscriptions
- Playlists
- Local content organization

Your TubeChief data is managed independently from your YouTube account.

## Local-First Approach

TubeChief follows a local-first approach:

- Your browser is the primary place where your data is stored.
- Core TubeChief functionality does not depend on creating a TubeChief account.
- Local preferences remain under your control.

## Supported Browsers

TubeChief is designed for Chromium-based browsers, including:

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Brave
- Other compatible Chromium browsers
