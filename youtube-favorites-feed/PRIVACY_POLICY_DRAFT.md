# Privacy Policy Draft

YouTube Favorites Feed does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

The extension stores the following data locally in the user's browser:
- favorite YouTube channel IDs and titles
- public video feed entries fetched from YouTube RSS feeds
- seen/unseen video status
- extension settings such as language, refresh interval, and notification preference

This data is stored using the browser's local extension storage. It is not sent to the developer or to any third-party server.

The extension does not request access to the user's Google or YouTube account and does not perform real YouTube subscriptions. It only reads public YouTube pages and public YouTube RSS feeds to build a local favorites feed.

Notifications are optional and are generated locally by the browser when new public feed entries are detected.
